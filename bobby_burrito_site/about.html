Our story

Family-owned. Proudly Canadian. Serious about flavour.

Family-owned in Windsor

Bobby Burrito & Taco was built with one simple idea: serve the kind of Mexican street food we love to eat, with the warm hospitality you expect from a family-run restaurant.

We’re proudly Canadian, rooted in Windsor and focused on being a neighbourhood spot where regulars are greeted by name.

Our food

From burritos and bowls to tacos, nachos and churros, everything starts with quality ingredients – fresh vegetables, bold spices and proteins prepared with care.

You choose your base, protein and toppings and we build it your way: regular, large or extra large burritos, hearty bowls, grilled quesadillas and fully-loaded nacho bowls.

Our community

We’re committed to being a positive part of East Windsor – from feeding busy families and students to supporting local events. Every order helps us grow, hire locally and keep bringing new flavours to the city.

Thank you for supporting a local, independent restaurant instead of another big chain.
