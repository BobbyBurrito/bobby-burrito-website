Who We Are

Bobby Burrito & Taco is a family-owned Mexican restaurant located in East Windsor. We started with a simple goal — bring real, bold, street-style Mexican flavours to the community, made fresh every day, using ingredients we can proudly stand behind.

From burritos and bowls to tacos, quesadillas and nachos, every dish is handcrafted and customizable. We don’t cut corners. We don’t use shortcuts. We make food the right way — fresh on the spot and loaded with flavour.
