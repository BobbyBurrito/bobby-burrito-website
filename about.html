About Bobby Burrito & Taco

Proudly Canadian-owned • Locally operated • Serving Windsor with flavour

A Proudly Canadian, Local Restaurant in Windsor

Bobby Burrito & Taco is a locally owned, Canadian restaurant built right here in Windsor, Ontario. We’re not a big chain and we’re not backed by investors — we’re a neighbourhood spot created by people who genuinely love good food and great service.

Our goal is simple: bring bold, fresh Mexican-inspired flavours to Windsor while supporting our local community, local jobs, and local families.

Fresh, Build-Your-Own Mexican Food

At Bobby Burrito & Taco, everything is made to order. From burritos and burrito bowls to tacos, quesadillas, nacho bowls and salads — you choose the base, protein and toppings, and we build it your way.

We focus on fresh ingredients, generous portions, and consistent quality, whether you’re grabbing a quick lunch, feeding your family, or ordering catering for a group.

Why Windsor Supports Bobby Burrito & Taco

Windsor is our home, and our customers are the reason we’re here. From students and workers to families and regulars, we’re proud to serve people who value honest food at fair prices.

When you eat at Bobby Burrito & Taco, you’re supporting a Canadian small business, local employment, and a restaurant that reinvests back into the Windsor community.

More Than Just Burritos

We’re constantly working to improve — new menu items, better service, and more ways to give value to our customers. From Taco Tuesday deals to catering platters and loyalty rewards, we’re here for everyday meals and special occasions alike.

Thank you for choosing Bobby Burrito & Taco and for supporting a local, Canadian-owned restaurant in Windsor.
